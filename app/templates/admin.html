How policy escalation works

  • Role violation+18 risk and a minimum Restrict decision.
  • Missing MFA+20 risk and a minimum Challenge decision.
  • Device below trust floor+15 risk and a minimum Challenge decision.
  • Hard controlsSuspended identity, malicious IP or device trust below 20 always blocks.

Policy evaluation coverage

Why policies and scoring must work together

Risk scoring alone cannot express an organisational prohibition. Least-privilege policies therefore act as a second, deterministic layer: they can raise the score, force a minimum outcome, and are recorded against every event for audit. Administrators can toggle a policy without a restart; the next request picks it up.
