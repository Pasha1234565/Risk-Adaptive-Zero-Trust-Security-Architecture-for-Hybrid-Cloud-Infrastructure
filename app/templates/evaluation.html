How to read these results

Detection treats Challenge, Restrict and Block as a positive response; containment counts only Restrict and Block. False-positive rate is measured on legitimate sessions, false-negative rate on the labelled attack corpus. Anomaly detection is scored separately at the model threshold. Latency figures are real wall-clock measurements in this single process and are indicative rather than production SLAs. The normal corpus is synthetic and seeded for reproducibility.
